## i01 — «חשבונית נכנסה למייל — ושורה הופיעה בטבלת ההוצאות» — FIX

**1. Is what is ours real?** Mostly yes, and it is specific: a dated run (2026-11-05, 12:32–12:40 UTC), token counts, the fields it wrote, and a demo invoice marked as a demo.
- **Cost recomputed from the official price page:** $0.016 is correct. On the deepseek-flash off-peak rates the run costs:
  - 1,220,224 cache-hit tokens × $0.003/M = $0.0037;
  - 43,994 cache-miss tokens × $0.15/M = $0.0066;
  - 9,954 output tokens × $0.6/M = $0.0060;
  - total **$0.0162**.
- **Off-peak is right for that time:** 12:32 UTC on a Thursday is outside peak hours (01–04 and 06–10 UTC, Mon–Fri).
- **The arithmetic is consistent:** 1,169.49 × 18% = 210.51, and 1,169.49 + 210.51 = 1,380.00. «Seven and a half minutes» fits the 12:32–12:40 window.
- **Stretched:**
  - **It is one run on one demo invoice.** The claim «מכניס חשבונית … נכון ובזול» reads as a general property.
  - **Hook 3, «מילא את טבלת ההוצאות לבד», says more than happened.** The agent was given a sentence, and it wrote a new CSV; it did not fill an existing table on its own.
  - **«Every field right» can only be checked inside staging.**

**2. Is it true to its sources?**
- The DeepSeek page supports the price, but only at off-peak. At peak the same run is **$0.032**, so «1.6 סנט» is a best-case price, and hook 2 states it as *the* price.
- `our_difference` says the others don't run «on the business's own server». The reasoning step runs on DeepSeek's cloud API, so the invoice's supplier and amounts leave the server. The piece must name that step, as i04 already does. If it doesn't, the reel implies something local that isn't.

**3. Would it stop a scroll?** Yes.
- Hooks 1 and 2 are concrete and natural.
- The honest slow number is the strongest line, and no hook uses it.
- «סנט» works, but an Israeli reader feels agorot. Use «פחות מ-6 אגורות» only with the day's Bank of Israel rate as a source.

**4. FACTS.md:** nothing it says is in «Never claim». But FACTS.md's own rule is «Never write a number about DGO that is not in this file», and $0.016 and 7.5 minutes are not in it.

**Fixes before the owner sees it:**
1. **Claim:** «בריצה אחת שמדדנו, סוכן ה-AI של הלשכה הכניס חשבונית מהמייל לטבלה, כל שדה נכון, ב-1.6 סנט (בשעות השפל) — אבל לא מהר: שבע וחצי דקות.»
2. **Hook 2:** «… 1.6 סנט, בשעות השפל.» Or give the range: «1.6–3.2 סנט».
3. **Hook 3:** replace it with the slow number, for example «הסוכן הכניס חשבונית לטבלה. זה לקח שבע וחצי דקות.»
4. **Name the cloud step in the piece:** the model is DeepSeek's API, paid per use. Drop «runs on the business's own server» from the difference.
5. **Proof the owner can open:** add the run's two numbers to FACTS.md («Measured, our own machines», DGO-661) first, and attach the session log and the CSV to the item.

## i03 — «מה להריץ על המחשב של העסק ומה לשכור: מדדנו דיבור, קריינות וסוכן» — BACK

**1. Is what is ours real?** No. The evidence doesn't hold as worded.
- **The transcription number is misread, in the wrong direction.** `apps/apitools/AGENT.md` (line 37–38) says the run was:
  - «~3× slower than real time for dense speech (RTF 0.29–0.35)»;
  - «about real time on an ordinary voice note»;
  - a 30-second note is «~20–40 s of work».

  The container is now capped at 1 CPU, and the doc says to «treat these as a floor». The doc's RTF is inverted: there, 0.3 means *slower*. Placed beside «faster than real time» TTS, «RTF 0.29–0.35» reads as fast, which is the opposite of the source.
- **The BlueTTS figure has no artifact.** I found no record of «25.4 s of speech in 14.6 s on 1.5 vCPU» anywhere in the repo. It is one sample.
- **No measurement was taken on the HP.** The evidence says «The HP office (6 cores, 9 GB, no GPU) is the business-class box», and `our_difference` says «every number is measured on a business-class machine». Neither is true: the numbers come from a 2-vCPU host, vps1 and an API price. The HP's specs are not in FACTS.md either.
- **The LLM half of the split has no number of ours.** The claim is «ולשכור את מודל השפה … והמספרים שלנו מראים למה», but no local model was measured. Ollama is in DGO per FACTS.md, so it could have been. The $0.016 is a price, not a comparison.
- **Nothing supports «משתלם» (pays).** The idea gives no cloud speech-to-text or TTS price to compare with.

**2. Is it true to its sources?** The sources themselves check out:
- the DeepSeek prices;
- BlueTTS is MIT, both the repo and its two Hugging Face weight repos (`notmax123/BlueTTS2.5-onnx`, `notmax123/blue-onnx-v2`);
- the ivrit.ai models are Apache-2.0 (the datasets use the ivrit.ai License v2).

But none of them carries the claim's economics, and the one piece of evidence of ours that has a source contradicts the idea.

**3. Would it stop a scroll?**
- Hook 1 («תמלול וקריינות בעברית רצים אצלכם. מודל השפה — לא.») has good rhythm. But «מודל השפה — לא» is a verdict without a number, and it cuts against FACTS.md: an office «may use outside AI providers or run local models», and Ollama ships in DGO.
- Hook 3 holds for TTS only, and only if the BlueTTS run is shown. «שתי ליבות» is really 1.5 vCPU capped on a 2-vCPU VPS.

**4. FACTS.md:**
- **Speech isn't in FACTS.md.** It lists «כלי מסמכים» (apitools) as OCR with Tesseract inside, with no speech-to-text, and BlueTTS and ivrit.ai Whisper aren't on its list of open-source parts. «רצים אצלכם» is therefore a claim about DGO that FACTS.md doesn't hold. It needs `[[fact: …]]` or a FACTS.md entry first.
- **Its numbers aren't there either.** The same «no number about DGO outside this file» rule applies to all three of i03's numbers.

**Why BACK and not FIX:** the core measurement points the wrong way, and half the split is unmeasured. Repairing it means new measurements, not new wording. Step 1 needs:
- **On the HP:** ivrit.ai turbo-ct2 on a 60-second voice note and on dense speech at the current cap; BlueTTS on the same text; a local model through Ollama, measured in tokens per second and in the time for i01's invoice task.
- **From the outside:** one cloud speech-to-text price from its primary page.

Then the claim can be written from the numbers, whichever way they fall.
