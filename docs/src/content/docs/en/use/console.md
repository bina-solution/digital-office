## Why

The centre is where you open every app of the office. It is worth getting to know once, and after that every app opens the same way.

## Before you start

You signed in to the office ([After the install](/en/start/after-install)) and you see the centre.

## The steps

1. **Open an app from the menu.** The side menu is split into four groups: «Main», «Business», «Infrastructure» and «Settings». Click an app, and it opens inside the centre, at an address of its own. For example, «Files» opens at `#/m/drive`. An app that runs in a [linked office](/en/concepts/link) carries the mark of that office.
2. **Search instead of scrolling.** `Ctrl K` (on a Mac `Cmd K`) opens search: type part of the name and open the app. `Esc` closes it.
3. **Go full screen.** The «Full screen» button hides the shell and leaves only the app. The «Exit full screen» button brings it back.
4. **On a phone there is a bar at the bottom** with seven places:
   - «Home»;
   - four apps;
   - «Open», with the number of apps open right now, to get back to what you were doing in one tap;
   - «More».

   An app gets a place in the bar when it asks for one in its declaration, `app.json`.
5. **On a wide screen you can switch to desktop mode.** Turn it on in the centre's settings screen: «Desktop mode», on screens 1024 pixels wide or more.
   - Each app opens in a window of its own, and the middle of the screen has a focus area.
   - A window dragged to one of the sides shrinks, but stays alive and running.
   - Shaking a window sends all the others to the sides.
   - The desktop's bottom bar shows as many apps as fit in it. The rest open from «More», which shows «All apps» with search.

   Shortcuts in desktop mode:

   | Key | What it does |
   |---|---|
   | `Ctrl K` | Search for and open an app |
   | `Alt` and `` ` `` | Switch between windows |
   | `Shift` while dragging | Snap to half the screen, or maximise |
   | `?` | Show or hide the shortcuts card |

## How to check it worked

- `Ctrl K` opens search, and an app opens from there.
- On a phone, «Open» shows what you opened.
