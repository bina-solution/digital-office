# The Digital Office: what it is, in the owner's words

The source for every sentence the Digital Office and Bina Solutions say about themselves: the two websites,
the business plan, the investor deck. When a page and this file disagree, the page is wrong.

The owner's words are kept verbatim below, and the concept and the requirements are drawn from them. The research and the
debate behind the story are in `research_notes/Digital Office positioning and debate/`, and their
synthesis is in `reports/Digital Office positioning and debate.md`.

## The concept (drawn from the owner's words, 2026-11-02)

1. **Not "an OS for a business", but an operating system for the modern era.** It sits above whatever
   device you hold (Mac, Windows, Linux, a phone) and goes around that layer: what you run no longer
   depends on which OS a machine has.
2. **All your computers, one centre.** It links every computer you own or rent (laptops, a strong
   home server, a cloud VPS) into one place of management and control (מרכז ניהול ובקרה), with
   guaranteed access from anywhere: from the phone you reach every app running on every one of your
   offices.
3. **Near-zero cost, because it is your compute.** You pay for the compute and nothing else. The price
   is transparent, and it is the same whether you run it on your own machines or in the cloud.
4. **The whole market of open-source apps, at a click.** It starts with a collection we curate and grow,
   and the end state is generic: any open-source system that has been or will be developed can be
   installed. "You get superpowers with your computers."
5. **Centralised, secure, and a place to create.** You develop and build things inside your Digital
   Office, not only consume apps.
6. **A literal digital office.** The name is meant literally: everything an office is, digital and yours.
7. **For whom?** A business is the clearest example of why this is needed, but it is not the only one:
   - a real-estate agent: accounting (Hashavshevet), Claude/AI, tasks, property management, media, leads;
   - commerce: inventory, purchasing, a shop and a web store, staff, products, media;
   - a service company;
   - a content creator;
   - a private person with no business: bills, online orders, AI, creative work, media, photos, a
     lifetime of files, all organised;
   - in short, anyone who wants to use technology and computing without its limits.
8. **By Bina Solutions.** The Digital Office says it comes from Bina Solutions, and Bina Solutions says
   it builds the Digital Office. Each links to the other, and the Digital Office links to its GitHub.

## What he asked for (the acceptance)

- The site is accurate first: it must stop telling a story that is narrower than the product.
- The business plan (vision.html) is accurate too.
- bina-solutions.co.il says that the company builds the Digital Office, and links to it.
- The research is deep, and it is run as a debate: at least 10 businesses, investors, governments and
  sceptics, Israel and the world. It is honest, and the conclusion is reached, not assumed.
- All the text is organised inside the DGO.
- Then the story is told at the level of an art piece ("Hollywood level"): the whole digital presence of
  Bina Solutions and the Digital Office, "a respectful and respected front".

**Rules this adds to the copy:**
- Hebrew only: ווינדוס, מק, לינוקס, אנדרואיד, אייפון. Never Latin names in the Hebrew text. The one
  exception is the brand mark: «DIGITAL OFFICE» stays in the header («No!! Digital office in header is ok
  of course»), next to «מבית בינה סולושנס».
- Talk about the product, never about our own machines or offices.
- Platforms: today ווינדוס, לינוקס and מק; אנדרואיד and אייפון are on the way.
- No pricing slogans in the hero.
- **No AI slop** («get rid of all!!! of the ai slop evrywhere», 19:34):
  - no emoji as icons or decoration, and no tinted emoji boxes;
  - no Latin jargon: «כניסה אחת», not SSO; «זיהוי טקסט», not OCR; «בינה מלאכותית», not AI;
    «אוטומציות», not n8n, in running text;
  - no stock constructions («בלי X, בלי Y», «לא X, אלא Y», «מקצה לקצה», three-adjective lists);
  - no em-dash rhythm;
  - every claim true today, and what is not built yet says «בקרוב».
  
  See issues/digital-office-site-reads-as-ai-slop.yaml.
- The story's arc, in his order:
  1. "your way to a smarter business"; an open-source space for running a business from A to Z.
  2. Where does your business live, and where does all its information sit? Most of office work is
     moving information between systems (the 90% figure must be checked against research before it is
     printed).
  3. The vision of a digital office in the AI era.
  4. The challenge: organising everything and bringing mature systems to market.
  5. The gap: solutions already exist for every office need, but there is no place to install them at a
     click and no office shell around them.
  6. Disorganisation makes processes expensive and slow.
  7. Full organisation, everything in one place, lets AI deliver its full potential inside a clear
     digital office: an app for everything, and a collection of open-source apps that are translated and
     ready.
  8. Together this means full digitisation of a business, in the cloud or on its own computers, free,
     within days, until the business moves forward by itself.
